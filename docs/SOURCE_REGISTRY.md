## Authority and use rules

1. Current official IOCL recruitment documents govern exam pattern, marking, dates, vacancies, and broad syllabus.
2. The official GATE 2026 CS syllabus may only expand the ten broad IOCL domain headings.
3. Historical official GATE papers and keys may support carefully attributed practice content but do not define IOCL scope or pattern.
4. The personal CIL response record provides candidate baseline context only. It is not an IOCL source and must not seed the question bank.
5. A filename or placement under `sources/official/` does not cure a malformed file, prove reuse rights, or replace provenance review.
